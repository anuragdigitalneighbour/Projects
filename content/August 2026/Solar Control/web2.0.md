## Fortifying Glass Against Impact and Intrusion

Standard annealed glass, commonly found in older residential and commercial buildings, is highly dangerous when broken. It shatters into large, sharp shards that can cause severe injury. Furthermore, a broken window provides immediate, unimpeded access for intruders. Even modern tempered glass, while safer when broken, still fails completely upon impact, leaving the property entirely exposed to the elements and unauthorized entry.

The professional installation of [Safety & Security Window Film Christchurch](https://solarcontrol.co.nz/services/safety-security-window-film/) dramatically improves the structural integrity of existing glass. These thick, multi-layered polyester films are bonded to the interior surface using aggressive adhesives. In the event of an impact—whether from a severe storm, accidental collision, or an attempted break-in—the film holds the shattered glass firmly in place within the frame. This prevents dangerous shards from entering the building and creates a robust barrier that significantly delays and deters forced entry, protecting the occupants and assets inside.
