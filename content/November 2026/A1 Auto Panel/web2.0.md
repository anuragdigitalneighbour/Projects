## Specialized Repair Methodologies for Modern Fibreglass and Composites

Unlike traditional automotive sheet steel, composite materials such as fibreglass-reinforced plastics and sheet moulding compounds do not possess metal memory. When an external impact strikes a composite panel, the energy shatters internal resin bonds and causes multi-layered fibre delamination. Attempting to repair a composite crack with surface filler creates a weak joint that fractures under road vibration.

Professional technicians begin composite repair by grinding a tapered scarf joint along the fractured fracture zone at a twelve-to-one slope. Tapering the damage exposes underlying structural fibre plies, providing an expansive surface for chemical and mechanical adhesion. Technicians cut replacement woven fibreglass fabrics to mirror original fibre orientation, restoring directional strength.
