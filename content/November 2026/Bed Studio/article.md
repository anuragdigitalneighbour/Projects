The mattress serves as the structural foundation for nightly physical recovery, governing spinal alignment, circulation, and sleep depth. Choosing the correct firmness requires balancing gentle pressure relief at sensitive joints with robust skeletal support across the entire sleep cycle.

While ultra-plush models often allow the lower back to sag and extra-firm surfaces can induce painful shoulder tension, medium mattresses achieve an exceptional equilibrium.
