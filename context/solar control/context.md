# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-04

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Christchurch's most trusted window tinting company, providing residential and commercial window film solutions since 1967.
**What it does:** Installs premium window films for homes and businesses to reduce heat, block UV rays, enhance privacy (frosted/opaque), and improve glass safety.
**Product category:** Home Services / Commercial Property Maintenance (Window Tinting)
**Product type:** Service
**Business model:** B2B and B2C (quotes and installations for local homeowners and businesses).

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Office buildings, local retail stores, commercial property managers in Christchurch and surrounding areas.
**Decision-makers:** Homeowners, facility managers, office managers, architects.
**Primary use case:** Tinting windows to solve issues with sun glare, overheating rooms, fading furniture, or lack of privacy.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Make rooms cooler and more comfortable by reducing solar heat gain.
- Protect expensive furniture, carpets, and artwork from fading due to UV rays.
- Create privacy in bathrooms or ground-floor offices without losing natural light.
**Use cases:**
- Retrofitting a sunny living room to prevent summer overheating.
- Adding frosted film to glass office partitions for privacy.
- Upgrading existing windows with safety/security films.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Standard glass windows allow too much heat, glare, and UV radiation into buildings, making spaces uncomfortable and damaging interiors.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Blinds and curtains block the view and require manual operation.
- Air conditioning is expensive to run constantly to combat solar heat.
- Replacing entire windows with tinted glass is extremely costly.
**What it costs them:** High energy bills (cooling), ruined furniture (fading), uncomfortable living/working conditions.
**Emotional tension:** Frustration with glaring sun on TV/computer screens, anxiety about fading valuable items, discomfort from heat.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other local window tinting and film installation companies in Christchurch.
**Secondary:** Blind and curtain installers, awning providers.
**Indirect:** HVAC companies (solving the heat problem differently), DIY window tint kits.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Over 60 years of business experience (since 1967).
- "Christchurch's most trusted" local reputation.
- Premium quality films with lifetime warranties.
- Price match guarantee.
**How we do it differently:** Expert, clean, and efficient installation backed by decades of hands-on experience, plus free on-site consultations.
**Why that's better:** Ensures a bubble-free, long-lasting finish that DIY kits or inexperienced installers can't provide.
**Why customers choose us:** Trust in their longevity, the security of a lifetime warranty, and competitive pricing.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Is it too expensive? | We offer a price match guarantee and free consultations to ensure you get competitive pricing without compromising quality. |

**Anti-persona:** Renters who are not allowed to modify their homes (unless paying out of pocket with landlord permission), or people looking for automotive/car window tinting (the focus here is residential/commercial flat glass).

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Unbearable summer heat, realizing a couch has faded, constant glare on the computer screen.
**Pull:** The promise of a cooler, more comfortable room while preserving the outdoor view.
**Habit:** Just putting up with it or keeping curtains closed all day.
**Anxiety:** Worrying the film will peel, bubble, or look cheap over time.

**Words to avoid:** Car tinting (unless they do offer it, but primary focus seems to be residential/commercial).
**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Frosted Film | An opaque film that provides privacy while allowing light through (ideal for bathrooms/offices). |
| Solar Heat Gain | The increase in temperature in a space as a result of solar radiation. |
| Safety & Security Film | A thicker film designed to hold shattered glass together upon impact. |
