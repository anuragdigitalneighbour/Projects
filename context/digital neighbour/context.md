# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-05

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A comprehensive digital marketing and web development agency helping New Zealand businesses scale.
**What it does:** Provides end-to-end digital growth services including SEO, paid advertising, content marketing, web development, and social media strategy.
**Product category:** B2B Services / Digital Marketing Agency
**Product type:** Service / Retainer
**Business model:** B2B (monthly retainers and project-based fees for marketing services).

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Small to medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), local businesses, and growing brands in New Zealand.
**Decision-makers:** Founders, CEOs, Marketing Managers, and Business Owners.
**Primary use case:** A business needs to increase their online visibility, drive more qualified leads, or build a high-converting website.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Rank higher on Google to capture organic search traffic (SEO).
- Generate immediate leads and sales through targeted ad campaigns (Paid Ads).
- Build a professional, fast, and conversion-optimized website (Web Dev).
**Use cases:**
- A local trades business needing Local SEO to get more phone calls.
- An e-commerce store wanting to scale revenue via Facebook and Google Ads.
- A company needing a complete website overhaul and rebranding.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Businesses are invisible online, losing market share to competitors, or wasting money on fragmented, ineffective marketing efforts.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Hiring in-house is expensive and it's hard to find a single person who is an expert in SEO, Ads, and Web Dev.
- "Cheap" overseas agencies often deliver poor quality, zero communication, and bad results.
- Niche freelancers can't provide a holistic, integrated strategy.
**What it costs them:** Stagnant revenue, wasted ad spend, frustration, and lost time.
**Emotional tension:** Overwhelm from the constantly changing digital landscape, distrust of agencies due to past bad experiences.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other New Zealand digital marketing agencies (e.g., Firefly, Zeald, Pure SEO).
**Secondary:** Freelance marketers and web developers.
**Indirect:** DIY website builders (Wix, Squarespace) and DIY boosting of Facebook posts.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Holistic approach (SEO, Paid, Web, Content) all under one roof.
- Focus on scaling and tangible ROI, not just vanity metrics.
- Specialized offerings like penalty recovery and App Store Optimization alongside standard SEO.
**How we do it differently:** We act as an extension of your business, aligning our digital strategies directly with your revenue goals.
**Why that's better:** Prevents siloed marketing. The website is built to convert the traffic that the ads and SEO generate.
**Why customers choose us:** Proven local expertise, comprehensive service offerings, and a focus on measurable business growth.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| I've been burned by an agency before. | We focus on complete transparency, clear reporting, and tying our metrics directly to your business revenue, not just clicks. |
| We don't have the budget for a full agency. | We scale our services to fit your growth stage, starting with high-impact channels (like Local SEO or targeted paid ads) to prove ROI quickly. |

**Anti-persona:** Micro-businesses with zero marketing budget wanting guaranteed overnight results, or businesses unwilling to collaborate on strategy.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** The current agency isn't delivering leads, or the business owner is exhausted from trying to do the marketing themselves.
**Pull:** The promise of a scalable, stress-free system that reliably generates new business.
**Habit:** Staying with a mediocre agency because switching seems like a massive headache.
**Anxiety:** Fear of wasting more money without seeing a return on investment.
