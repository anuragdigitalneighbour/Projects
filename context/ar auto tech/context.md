# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-05

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Expert independent mechanic and auto repair workshop in New Lynn, Auckland.
**What it does:** Provides comprehensive car servicing, mechanical repairs, auto electrical diagnostics, WOF, and performance/4x4 upgrades.
**Product category:** Automotive Repair / Mechanic
**Product type:** Service
**Business model:** B2C and B2B (servicing everyday drivers, 4x4 enthusiasts, and WINZ clients).

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Local businesses with fleets, WINZ.
**Decision-makers:** Vehicle owners in West Auckland.
**Primary use case:** Routine vehicle maintenance, emergency breakdowns, or specialized performance upgrades.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Ensure the car passes its Warrant of Fitness (WOF) and is safe to drive.
- Fix sudden mechanical or electrical failures (brakes, transmission, engine lights).
- Upgrade a 4x4 or performance vehicle for better capability.
**Use cases:**
- A family car needing its annual service and WOF.
- Quoting and repairing a vehicle via WINZ assistance.
- A 4x4 enthusiast looking for suspension and engine upgrades.

| Persona | Cares about | Challenge | Value we promise |
|---------|-------------|-----------|------------------|

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Cars inevitably break down or require maintenance, causing severe disruption to daily life and stress over unexpected costs.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Dealerships charge exorbitant hourly rates for the same work.
- Cheap mechanics might lack the advanced diagnostic tools needed for modern cars.
**What it costs them:** Stress, loss of mobility to get to work/school, high repair bills.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of being overcharged, anxiety when a warning light comes on on the dashboard.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other independent mechanics and auto workshops in New Lynn and West Auckland.
**Secondary:** Franchise service centers (e.g., Pit Stop, Midas).
**Indirect:** DIY repairs, ignoring the problem until the car dies.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- 15+ years of experience.
- Official WINZ Supplier (free WINZ quotes).
- Specialized in both modern auto electrical diagnostics and performance/4x4 upgrades.
**How we do it differently:** Fully equipped with advanced diagnostic tools but operating independently to avoid dealership overhead.
**Why that's better:** You get direct communication with the mechanic working on your car, honest advice, and quality workmanship without the dealership price tag.
**Why customers choose us:** Trust in the local New Lynn community, comprehensive services (from AC repair to engine rebuilds), and acceptance of WINZ cards.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Can you fix modern/complex cars? | Absolutely. We are fully equipped with advanced diagnostic tools to handle modern vehicle electronics. |
| I can't afford this right now. | We are an official WINZ supplier, provide free WINZ quotes, and accept WINZ cards to help get you back on the road. |

**Anti-persona:** People looking for cheap, unsafe patch-jobs just to illegally pass a WOF.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Current mechanic is too expensive, slow, or failed to diagnose a complex electrical issue.
**Pull:** Dealership-level diagnostics at local mechanic prices, plus specialized 4x4 knowledge.
**Habit:** Returning to the same mechanic out of convenience even if the service is mediocre.
**Anxiety:** Fear that a new mechanic will invent problems to charge more money.

## Customer Language
**How they describe the problem:**
- "My check engine light is on."
- "My car is making a grinding noise."
- "I need a WOF and a basic service."
**How they describe us:**
- "A good local mechanic in New Lynn."
- "My car guy."
**Words to use:** Reliable, honest advice, dealership alternative, advanced diagnostics, local, WINZ supplier.
**Words to avoid:** Cheap fixes (focus on quality and value instead).
**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| WOF | Warrant of Fitness – New Zealand's mandatory safety inspection for vehicles. |
| WINZ Quote | A specialized quotation format required for clients seeking financial assistance from Work and Income NZ. |
