# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-05

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Reliable before-school, after-school, and holiday care programs for children in Auckland.
**What it does:** Provides structured, engaging, and safe childcare environments outside of standard school hours. Services include supervised play, creative activities, group games, and themed holiday trips.
**Product category:** Childcare / Out of School Care (OSCAR)
**Product type:** Service
**Business model:** B2C (fees paid by parents, often subsidized by WINZ/OSCAR).

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** N/A (B2C focused).
**Decision-makers:** Parents and guardians of school-aged children in Auckland.
**Primary use case:** Needing a safe and engaging place for children while parents are at work before or after school, or during school holidays.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Ensure children are safe and supervised outside of school hours.
- Keep children engaged with fun, educational, and creative activities.
- Provide reliable childcare coverage that aligns with working parents' schedules.
**Use cases:**
- Dropping kids off early before the workday begins.
- After-school care until parents finish work.
- Full-day holiday programs during school breaks.

## Personas
| Persona | Cares about | Challenge | Value we promise |
|---------|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| Working Parent | Child safety, reliable hours, cost (subsidies) | Juggling strict work hours with school schedules | Peace of mind knowing their child is safe, happy, and supervised |

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Standard school hours don't align with traditional working hours, leaving parents stressed about childcare logistics.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Hiring a private nanny or babysitter is extremely expensive.
- Relying on family members isn't always reliable or possible.
- Unstructured care can leave kids bored and inactive.
**What it costs them:** Stress, missed work hours, high alternative childcare costs.
**Emotional tension:** Guilt about not being there after school, anxiety about child's safety and happiness.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other local OSCAR-approved after-school and holiday programs (e.g., SKIDS, Kelly Club).
**Secondary:** Private babysitters, local community center drop-in programs.
**Indirect:** Adjusting work hours, working from home while managing kids.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Operates out of multiple convenient Auckland school locations (Flanshaw Road, Jireh Christian, Anchorage, Riverhills, Elim Christian, St Mary's).
- OSCAR approved (eligible for WINZ subsidies).
- Engaging, themed activities rather than just "babysitting."
**How we do it differently:** A structured yet fun environment that feels like a club rather than a waiting room.
**Why that's better:** Kids actually want to attend, removing the daily battle for parents.
**Why customers choose us:** Convenience of location, affordability via subsidies, and a proven track record of safe, engaging care.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Is it affordable? | We are fully OSCAR approved, meaning many families qualify for WINZ subsidies to significantly reduce the cost. |
| Will my child be bored? | We have structured, creative, and themed activities designed to keep kids actively engaged and having fun. |

**Anti-persona:** Parents looking for strict academic tutoring programs (this is focused on care, play, and engagement).

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Current childcare arrangement fell through, or kids are complaining about being bored at a different program.
**Pull:** A convenient location right at their child's school with fun activities.
**Habit:** Struggling through the current routine because switching paperwork seems daunting.
**Anxiety:** Worrying if their child will make friends or like the new staff.
