# BedStudio Brief

## Purpose

BedStudio is the new premium, showroom-led brand for the client. It should be built as its own website and local visibility asset, not as a simple copy or redirect of Budget Beds.

## Brand Role

BedStudio should stand for:

- premium beds, mattresses, bases, and headboards
- NZ-made and customisable options
- showroom-led advice in Newmarket
- better trust and service experience than a generic online-only store
- practical support with delivery, setup, warranty, payment options, and after-sales questions

## Relationship To Budget Beds

Budget Beds remains the value/ecommerce brand.

BedStudio should be positioned separately:

- Budget Beds: affordability, ecommerce history, value-led search demand.
- BedStudio: Newmarket showroom, premium trust, NZ-made/custom products, guided buying experience.

The 9 July 2026 meeting notes indicate the client wants the two brands kept clearly separate to avoid customer confusion. This should be confirmed before any public cross-linking decision, because controlled cross-linking may still have SEO or user-experience value if handled carefully.

## Primary Website Goal

Get a credible BedStudio web presence live quickly, then expand it into a full ecommerce/showroom site.

The minimum viable version should:

- make BedStudio look real and trustworthy from launch
- show what categories are sold
- explain the Newmarket showroom and local experience
- collect calls/enquiries
- support launch signage, social traffic, Google searches, and walk-in validation

## SEO Priorities

1. Newmarket local visibility.
2. Auckland showroom and category searches.
3. NZ-wide delivery/product searches where relevant and truthful.

## Conversion Priorities

- Make the showroom visit feel worthwhile.
- Help customers choose by size, feel, sleeper type, and product category.
- Make delivery, warranty, returns, and payment options clear.
- Use launch offers carefully, without overpromising.
- Build trust through photos, product detail, reviews, service process, and clear FAQs.
